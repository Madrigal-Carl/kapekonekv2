## What we collect

Your name, username, email, phone number, delivery addresses, orders and receipts. For farmers: farm locations, tree and harvest records, and bean listings. We also keep a record of important actions (the audit log) to keep accounts safe.

## Why we use it

To run your account, deliver orders, confirm payments, let Group Managers and Kaluppa support farmers, and send the emails you need (verification, orders, password resets).

## Who can see it

Kaluppa staff. Your Group Manager, for farmers in their association. Other users only see your name, role and what you post in the Hub. We don’t sell your data.

## How long we keep it

While your account is active, and afterwards only as long as records must be kept for orders and payments.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Send the request through the Contact us page.
