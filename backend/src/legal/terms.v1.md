## 1. Using KapeKonek

KapeKonek is Kaluppa’s marketplace for Marinduque coffee. Buyers order roasted coffee and other products. Farmers record their farms and sell beans to Kaluppa. Group Managers run their farmer associations. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

## 2. Your account

Give your real name and an email address you can open. Keep your password private; you are responsible for what happens under your account. Tell Kaluppa right away if you think someone else used it. Accounts made by Kaluppa or a Group Manager start with a default password that you should change after signing in.

## 3. Orders and payment

Prices, stock and delivery fees are shown before you place an order. Kaluppa confirms each order within 3 days; an order that is not confirmed in time expires, and any e-wallet payment is refunded. Upload a clear receipt for e-wallet payments. Cash orders are paid on delivery or pickup.

## 4. Selling beans as a farmer

Listings must describe your own beans truthfully: variety, form, weight and price. Until your association approves you, you can sell up to 5 kg. Kaluppa may reject a listing with a reason, and a sale is only complete once Kaluppa weighs and pays for the beans at the branch.

## 5. Community Hub and messages

Be respectful. Don’t post false information, other people’s private details, or offers to sell outside KapeKonek. Kaluppa may remove posts, comments or reviews that break these rules and will tell you why.

## 6. Reviews

You can review a product only after you have ordered it. Reviews must be your honest experience. You can edit or delete your own reviews.

## 7. Changes and contact

Kaluppa may update these terms. When they change in a way that matters, you will be asked to agree again the next time you sign in. Questions? Use the Contact us page.
